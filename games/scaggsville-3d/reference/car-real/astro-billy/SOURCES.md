# Billy's Van — Real Reference Photos: Gen-1 Chevrolet Astro

## The van (single vehicle, single photo shoot)

- **Year / model:** 1990 Chevrolet Astro — passenger van (NOT cargo), short-wheelbase RWD
- **Generation:** First generation (1985–1994) — confirmed visually: flat/short nose, sealed-beam rectangular headlights, horizontal-slat grille with centre bowtie, barn (bi-parting) rear doors. This is NOT the 1995–2005 gen-2.
- **VIN (as listed):** 1GNDM15Z7LB106805
- **Colour (as listed):** Blue — two-tone: light blue upper/lower with a dark blue centre band; blue cloth interior
- **Mileage (as listed):** 272,470 miles — cross-confirmed in `detail-cluster.jpg`, where the odometer reads 272470
- **Engine / transmission (as listed):** 4.3L V6, automatic
- **Equipment (as listed):** power windows, power door locks, cruise control (window/lock switches visible in `detail-door-panel.jpg`)
- **Trim:** not stated in the listing
- **Location at listing:** Orange, California, USA — California plate 3SML551 (visible in front/rear shots, matches the listing's licence note)
- **Condition note:** paint is faded/peeling in places — irrelevant for this set; panels, glass, trim lines and proportions are all clearly readable.
