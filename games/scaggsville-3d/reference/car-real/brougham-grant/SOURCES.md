## Colour caveat
**Reference is for SHAPE only** — panels, glass, vinyl roof, chrome, wheels. This example is black/silver; **canon paint for Grant's car is MAROON**, applied on top of this shape. Do not copy the black paint, gold grille/badging, or red pinstripe as canon unless the design says so.

## Verification notes
- Every saved file was viewed and confirmed to be the squared-off RWD Brougham sedan (long hood, formal vinyl-roof C-pillar, vertical taillights) — NOT a front-wheel-drive DeVille/Fleetwood of the same years.
- One gallery-adjacent CDN file (/1.jpg) was a generic stock photo of modern cars, not this Cadillac — it was excluded and not saved.
