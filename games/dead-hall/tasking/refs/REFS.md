# Dead Hall — Staging Reference Frames

One frame per room. These are **composition references only** — staging, framing and
lighting to build to. Every figure in the game itself is an ORIGINAL build; do not
copy a likeness, costume or character design from these stills. Match the *shot*:
where the figure stands, where the light comes from, how much of the frame is dark.

## 01.jpg — Hallway twins (The Shining)
Source: https://fr.pinterest.com/pin/classic-cinema--133489576450132980/
- Perfectly symmetrical one-point corridor, camera dead center at standing eye height; the pair stands small at the far end, centered on the vanishing point.
- Even, flat ceiling-pendant light the whole length — no shadows to hide in; the dread is the stillness and the symmetry.
- Figures hold hands, feet together, facing camera; wallpaper + door rhythm repeats identically on both sides.

## 02.jpg — Doorway woman (P.T.)
Source: https://yoqqukos.pages.dev/posts/games-that-deconstruct-horror/
- Figure stands motionless just in front of a black open doorway at the end of a short domestic hall, centered, full-length, facing camera.
- Single overhead light source above/behind camera: face lit pale from above, dress falls into darkness below the waist, long shadow thrown back into the doorway.
- Clutter stays at the frame edges (sideboard, picture frames, window) — the center of the hall is empty except for her.

## 03.jpg — EXIT-sign silhouette (Outlast)
Source: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2882928084
- Full figure reduced to a pure silhouette, backlit through frosted glass doors; hands pressed flat to the glass at shoulder height, legs braced wide.
- The only saturated color in frame is the EXIT sign glowing directly above the figure's head; sickly green backlight behind the glass, red-orange spill on the walls.
- Camera square-on to the doors, figure centered — read the body outline, zero facial detail.

## 04.jpg — Night-vision crawler ([REC])
Source: https://groovelite.pages.dev/posts/15-best-horror-movies-of-the-2000s-ranked/
- Whole frame is night-vision green monochrome with hard falloff to black at the edges — the light is the camera's own IR, so brightness dies within a few meters.
- Figure on all fours at floor level, crawling toward camera, one hand reaching forward into the foreground; camera sits low, near the floor.
- Wet hair hangs over the face; one eye catches the IR and glints. Nothing else in the room is visible.

## 05.jpg — Corner figure (Blair Witch)
Source: https://independent.academia.edu/%C5%81ukaszSchwartz
- Figure seen only from behind, standing in the corner where two rough stone walls meet, face to the wall, arms hanging slack.
- Grainy black-and-white camera light from behind the viewer: the figure's own shadow is thrown onto the wall in front of him.
- He stands slightly right of center, motionless; the empty left of frame is bare stone — the composition is mostly wall.

## 06.jpg — Figure out of the well / floor hatch (The Ring)
Source: https://visualfoodie.com/who-played-samara-the-ghost-character/
- Camera looks straight down into a circular stone opening; the figure climbs up out of it toward the lens, so she grows out of the center of a dark ring.
- Cold blue light from above (the camera's side); the shaft walls and below her are pure black.
- Long wet hair curtains the face completely; both hands grip the rim at 9 and 3 o'clock, fingers splayed and clawed.

## 07.jpg — Mascot in the hall (FNAF — the sprint room)
Source: Screen Rant image CDN (static0.srcdn.com), FNAF office/hallway still
- Mascot stands at the far end of a black-and-white checkered hall, framed inside a doorway, centered on the hall's axis.
- One hanging lamp above the doorway is the only light — figure lit from above, the near hall and office foreground fall off to black.
- Figure upright and still, one arm raised (hook hand) — this is the beat *before* the run: small in frame, full body visible, long empty checkered floor between it and camera.

## 08.jpg — Figure in the doorway (FNAF — Bonnie)
Source: https://mungart.com/search/Bonnie%20in%20Doorway%20F-NaF
- Figure leans out from behind a doorframe, half in / half out of the dark: head and one shoulder past the jamb, the rest still in blackness.
- Face is a black void — the only features are two small pale pinprick eyes. Muzzle catches a rim of cold light from the room side.
- Camera is close (the doorway fills the frame) with the DOOR/LIGHT panel at frame left as a scale anchor; in Dead Hall, stage it at the moment the opened door reveals him.

## 09.jpg — Tall figure (Poppy Playtime)
Source: Civitai image CDN (image.civitai.com), Huggy Wuggy hallway render
- Impossibly tall, lanky figure fills a doorway/hall from floor to near the ceiling: legs and arms far too long, head small at the top of frame.
- Backlit: a warm doorway glow behind the figure rims the fur/limbs and pools on the floor between its feet; the front of the body stays near-silhouette.
- Portrait composition — camera low, looking slightly up, figure centered and front-on, feet planted wide at the bottom corners of frame.

## 10.jpg — Eye-wall chase (Roblox Doors — Seek)
Source: https://dotesports.com/roblox/news/how-to-beat-seek-in-roblox-doors
- Long hotel corridor in alarm-red light, shot from just behind the fleeing player — the chase reads down the hall's perspective lines.
- Eyes are embedded in the walls themselves at irregular heights, all staring into the hall; black tendril arms reach out of the walls toward the runner.
- A bright hazard glow (fire) blocks the far end — the runner runs *toward* light while the dark behind produces the threat.

## 11.jpg — Spring-neck mannequin (Lethal Company — Coil-Head)
Source: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?l=romanian&id=3085981623
- Full-body figure study: smooth pale mannequin body, bolt heads studding the torso and limbs, standing slack with arms slightly away from the body.
- The head floats well above the shoulders on a long exposed coil spring — the neck gap is roughly a head-and-a-half of bare spring, head tilted, mouth open.
- In the room, stage it dead still in flat light like this study; the scare is that it is closer every time the light comes back.

## 12.jpg — Yellow room (Backrooms)
Source: https://cinemachaos.substack.com/p/backrooms
- Empty mono-yellow office maze: patterned yellow wallpaper, yellow carpet, drop-tile ceiling with humming fluorescent panels; no figure, no furniture, no exit in frame.
- Flat, even fluorescent light with almost no shadow — the opposite of every other room; eye-level camera, pillars and half-walls repeat into the distance.
- The composition is pure liminal repetition: any one doorway looks like every other, and nothing is coming.
