# ADDENDUM 2 to OPUS-FIX-PLAY-VISIBILITY — defect 6: screen presence during action
Hospital Escape · Cherie, Nov 23 (third play note)

## 6. During action, we see too much of them — a fraction is enough
The characters' full bodies block too much of the screen while the
fighting is happening. Seeing the full figure is fine for a brief
split-scene moment (a cutscene beat, an establishing shot) — it is
NOT fine during action.

Rule: **during action, an enemy occupies only a fraction of the
frame** — partial body, or full body small enough that the scene
stays readable around them. They must still satisfy the fairness
contract (visible, unoccluded, identifiable as the shooter at
390×844) — identifiable, yes; screen-filling, no. The two rules
together: the player can always tell who is shooting, and can
always see the room around them.

How you get there (distance, placement, camera, engagement range)
is your call — but check it against the pace numbers: engagement
changes may not re-lengthen the game (~209 s / 5.7 s / ~122–126 s
watch-only still hold).

Proof: captures at action moments across the fighting beats,
before/after, with each active shooter's on-screen height noted as
a fraction of the 844 px frame. Cutscene/establishing shots may keep
the big full-body framing — mark which captures are which.
