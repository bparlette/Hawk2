The main cast (N2 gunmen, Maya, Cole standing) is first-class. The
remaining figures are not, and the owner will not ship them as they are:

1. **Dr. Hale is horrible.** He is a flat painted cutout standing in a
   3D scene. Replace him with a full rigged 3D character at the main
   cast's standard — or remove him. (Replacement is preferred: he has
   dialogue beats. If removal is chosen, say what happens to his
   lines.)
2. **The patient lying down is horrible** — the figure on the bed in
   the opening/ambulance image and the bed scenes reads flat and
   wrong next to the cast. Replace with the real rigged character
   lying properly on the bed.
3. **Same for the standard nurses / background staff.** Every figure
   in the game must be a first-class rigged asset like everyone else
   — no flat cutouts, no low-grade stand-ins.
4. **Bed push is wrong:** Maya pushes the bed **from the side with one
   hand**. She must be **behind the bed, pushing with two hands**.
   (This supersedes the earlier as-is approval of the bed-push clip:
   the clip is fine, the in-game staging is not.)
