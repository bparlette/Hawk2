## What game is this?

**Presented as:** GTA 6 — the two leads are Jason (man) and Lucia (woman), GTA 6's protagonists, and Part 1 is titled as a "Chapter 1 Finale."

**What the footage actually shows (high confidence):** the on-screen HUD is GTA V's — the bottom-left minimap design, the top-right cash + ammo counter with GTA V-style weapon icons, the subtitle font/styling, and the bottom-center bold objective text ("Defend yourself!") are all GTA V's mission presentation. Character models, lighting and the "[Unknown Language]" subtitle tag also match modded GTA V. **Best identification: a fan-made "GTA 6 concept" mission built in modded GTA V (PC), recorded and uploaded as GTA 6 content.** The names Jason/Lucia come from the uploaders' titles, not from any in-game title card — no title screen appears in either clip.

**Why it matters for the replica:** you don't need GTA 6. The design is a scripted, mostly on-rails third-person shooter mission with two characters who trade roles. Cherie's instinct — "the camera does all the work and you just aim and shoot" — is exactly right for the wheelchair and ambulance sections (details below).

## The cast and the roles (Cherie's read, verified and corrected)

Cherie read it as: *you rotate between the wheelchair PUSHER, a SHOOTER, and the PERSON IN THE WHEELCHAIR who shoots.* The footage says: **there are only two playable characters, and one of them covers two of those roles.**

1. **Jason — the patient / the man in the wheelchair.** Long dark hair (sometimes in a bun), beard, tinted glasses, a white-and-black patterned **camo bucket hat**, light-blue t-shirt (bloodstained as the mission goes on; a pale-green hospital gown in the final shot). He has been shot/stabbed and **cannot walk properly** — that's the whole premise. While seated in the chair he shoots a pistol (later a machine pistol) from chair height.
2. **Lucia — the pusher AND the second shooter.** Long braid, trucker/baseball cap, sunglasses, hoop earring, grey hoodie. She pushes Jason's wheelchair through the escape, fights beside him with her own pistol, and in the middle section **the player takes control of her on foot** (full HUD appears) while Jason is parked/defending. In Part 2 she **drives**.

So the rotation is: **play as Jason-in-the-chair (pusher is AI) → play as Lucia on foot → play as Jason defending from the chair → (Part 2) play as Jason shooting from the ambulance back while Lucia drives.** Switching is scripted by the mission (cutscene-to-gameplay cuts), not a free character wheel — no switch UI is visible in either clip.

No ramming by the ambulance is shown — the ambulance never turns around; **all kills are by gunfire from the back.**

## Camera — the heart of the design

1. **Wheelchair segments (Part 1, Jason):** camera locked at **seated height (~1 m)**, close over-the-shoulder/behind the chair, moving along a **fixed path** (Lucia pushes). The player has **aim + fire only**. Injury is sold with post-processing: strong vignette, edge blur, chromatic aberration, desaturation; damage adds a **red edge vignette**; near-death washes the frame **yellow-green**. This is Cherie's "the camera does all the work" — a rail shooter wearing a GTA costume.
2. **Lucia's section (Part 1, ~1:55–2:20):** standard **free third-person** over-shoulder with the full HUD — the one conventional segment, included so the mission doesn't feel like a pure rail ride.
3. **Holdout segment (Part 1, ~2:25):** camera essentially **static** behind the parked chair; waves come to you; aim + fire only.
4. **Ambulance segment (Part 2):** camera **fixed inside the rear of the moving ambulance**, looking back out of the open doors; the chase route is scripted; aim + fire only, with a generous aim arc (targets directly behind and pulling alongside).
5. **Cutscenes** bookend every segment (dialogue, wound check, weapon pickup, mirror check) — roughly a third of Part 1's runtime is cutscene.

## What the player actually does
- **Aim** with a small center reticle (thin circle; red X hitmarker in Part 2).
- **Shoot** (pistol → machine pistol in Part 1; automatic weapon in Part 2; Lucia additionally uses a rifle/SMG in her section).
- In Lucia's section only: **move, take cover, reload** like normal GTA (ammo counter visible, mag hits 0).
- **No driving, no steering, no character-switch input** is visible anywhere in either clip. Objective text tells you the job ("Defend yourself!"); dialogue tells you the story.

## Enemies & weapons
- **Part 1 enemies:** plain-clothes gunmen (one in an orange/red shirt) speaking an untranslated foreign language; later waves in light uniforms/scrubs. They use pistols/SMGs, take cover behind carts, counters and doorframes, and rush in waves during the holdout.
- **Part 2 enemies:** marked police cars (sedan + Charger-style with push bars) and unmarked black SUVs; 4-star response per the uploader's caption.
- **Player weapons seen:** pistol, machine pistol/compact SMG (the 0:55 pickup close-up), rifle/SMG (Lucia), automatic weapon (Part 2). Ammo counters in GTA format (mag / reserve).

## HUD / UI inventory
- **During rail segments:** almost nothing — subtitles, damage vignette, center reticle. Deliberately clean.
- **During Lucia's free section (Part 1):** GTA V layout — **minimap bottom-left** (with blips), **cash + ammo top-right** with weapon icon, **objective text bottom-center** (bold white), **subtitles bottom-center** above it. Health/armor are the small bars under the minimap in GTA style (present in layout; values not readable at 360p).
- **Part 2:** the portrait crop cuts most HUD off; visible in-frame are the **reticle + hitmarker** and subtitles. The wanted stars are asserted by the uploader's caption only.

## Art style
Photoreal-leaning GTA V with graphics mods: dim fluorescent hospital interiors, heavy atmosphere (smoke in the ward section), rain and wet reflections in Part 2, stormy dawn sky, strong post-processing (grain, vignette, chromatic aberration). Costumes are contemporary street clothes; the hospital is dressed with posters, signage ("keep this door clear"-type signs), directory boards, gurneys, carts and waiting chairs.

## Replica recipe (one-paragraph summary)
Build a scripted mission in three rail beats and one free beat: **(1)** AI companion pushes the wounded hero in a wheelchair along a corridor spline while the player aims/fires a pistol from a locked seated-height camera; upgrade his gun mid-mission; **(2)** hard-cut control to the companion for one free third-person room-clear with full HUD; **(3)** park the chair for a wave-defense holdout ("Defend yourself!"); **(4)** cut to the ambulance: fixed rear-facing camera in a moving vehicle on a scripted night route, AI companion driving, player shooting out pursuers' tires/drivers/engines until the chase "goes quiet," ending on a mirror-check cutscene. Keep HUD off during rail beats; damage = red vignette, near-death = sickly yellow-green wash.

## Cherie's replica structure — her design, not the original's

Cherie's own mission outline for the replica (this is her build spec layered on top of the analysis above — it differs from the original where she says so, most notably: the original never shows a hospital bed, an elevator ride as its own beat, the main-entrance exit, the ambulance theft, or a train ending):

1. **Start:** visiting him in his hospital bed; the friends enter the room.
2. **Wheel the entire BED down a hall or two** — the bed is the first vehicle.
3. **Transfer him into a wheelchair.**
4. **Elevator ride down.**
5. **Through many hallways, the cafeteria, and the waiting room.**
6. **Out the main entrance.**
7. **Steal the ambulance.**
8. **Chase:** shooter in the back, crashing the follower cars.
9. **Ending:** narrowly cross the train tracks — **the train traps all the pursuers on the other side.**
