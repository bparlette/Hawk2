# NEON — BAKE-OFF: ONE CAR, FROM SCRATCH (Nov 20, from Cherie via Moth)

**Work in `main`. Commit to `main`. No branches — ever.** (Standing rule for all tasking.)

## The assignment

A bake-off. Build **one car from scratch — your best possible** — based **only** on the real reference photos in `games/bunny-hop/reference/car-bar/`:

- **Hero angle:** `ute-shop-dusk.jpg` — the blue ute at the tiki auto shop at blue hour.
- **Side profile:** `drive-5.jpg` — the same car parked in profile, driver visible through the glass.
- The rest of the set (`ute-shop-night.jpg`, `shop-overhead.jpg`, `drive-1.jpg` … `drive-4.jpg`) is yours to study for light, wheels, and setting.

The car: a **car-pickup (ute)** — long hood, single cab, open bed, blue paint, chrome/whitewall-trim wheels, a driver seated inside.

**Clean sheet.** Not based on Moth's shared `gpvehicles.js` module. Not an edit of your Bunny Hop street cars. Start from the photo and build the best single car you can.

## Why

Cherie wants to see your ceiling on one car with no other constraints, to compare against:

1. the real photo, and
2. Moth's shared-module cars (Scaggsville 3D — the fleet got a car-bar upgrade today: clearcoat-style reflective paint, interiors with seated drivers, spoked wheels, light signatures).

## Deliverable

- The car in a small **standalone viewer page** you add at `games/bunny-hop/bakeoff-car/` — your own scene. A dusk grade, a hint of lit frontage, wet-look ground: fair game. The photo's setting sells the car, so set it.
- **Fixed screenshot angles matching the references**, captured at phone size (390×844):
  1. **3/4 rear-side** — the `ute-shop-dusk.jpg` angle.
  2. **Pure side profile** — the `drive-5.jpg` angle.
  3. **3/4 front.**
- Put the screenshots in the same folder and link them in your response file (`games/bunny-hop/tasking/responses/`).

## Judging

Side-by-side against the real photo at the same angles, at phone size:

1. **Shape first** — silhouette, proportions, panel flow. Does it read as *that* ute?
2. **Then finish** — paint, glass, wheels, lights.

In your response, state your **draw calls and frame cost** for the scene, and **what you deliberately left out** and why (the adopt / defer / rule-out honesty of your car-bar response is the model).

## Pace

No deadline games: **quality over speed** on this one. It's a ceiling test.
